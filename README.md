# A Boy in the Wild

![](/resources/media/images/a-boy-in-the-wild.webp)

A WordPress block theme unlike anything in the directory.

---

## What is this?

*A Boy in the Wild* is a serialized, episodic WordPress block theme built around a single ongoing story — a semi-autobiographical account of a boy who lives in the wilderness, told one chapter at a time.

Each chapter is a post. Each post has its own visual identity. The content drives the design — not the other way around.

This is not a theme that ships complete. It grows in public, one season at a time.

---

## The idea

Most WordPress themes are containers. You pour your content into them and hope for the best. *A Boy in the Wild* works differently. The design system responds to the story — to the season, the mood, the emotional register of what is being told. A chapter set during a storm looks and feels nothing like a chapter written in the warmth of late summer. The typography, the palette, the spacing, the texture of the page — all of it changes because the story demands it.

The theme is built on a simple conviction: **the content should decide the design.**

---

## The story

A boy. The wilderness. Mostly true.

He is twelve when the story starts. He is somewhere north of a ridge with no name, in a clearing he has decided is his. He has a fire — adequate, not the largest he has ever made. He has a map he drew from memory, with twelve things named and one location marked deliberately wrong. He has buried something he is not ready to talk about.

The writing is first-person. It is unreliable. There are passages crossed out. There are things he corrects later. There are things he never corrects.

The rest of the story is still being written.

---

## The theme

- A WordPress block theme, fully site-editor compatible
- A design token system built on block style variations — one per season, mood, and narrative arc
- A topographic map texture that shifts with the story
- Starter patterns for each chapter — pre-written, pre-styled, ready to publish
- A custom style engine that makes block style variations more powerful than WordPress natively allows
- Built to WordPress.org submission standards

---

## The format

New chapters arrive with the seasons. Each one introduces new block patterns and new design directions — new ways the theme can express what the story needs. The theme is intentionally incomplete at launch. That is not a limitation. That is the point.

Following this theme means following the story. The changelog is the arc.
