Unchartered

Lost · Somewhere in the trees · No landmarks visible

You've gone somewhere
I haven't charted.

This stretch isn't on anything I've drawn. I've walked into places like this — every direction looks like every other direction, the light the same everywhere. You stop. You listen. You work out which way the water runs.

The page you were looking for isn't here. But here isn't nothing. It's just unmapped. That's different. Home is back the way you came — it hasn't moved.

If you don't know where you are, find water. It goes somewhere. Most things do.
