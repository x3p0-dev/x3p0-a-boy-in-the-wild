## [Chapter 1: The Clearing] — 2026-05-23

### Story

- Day 1. Age 12. Late summer. The fire is adequate — not the largest he has ever made.
- The first release. Everything built from nothing. The field note register established, the parchment and ink palette set, the design system assembled around a single conviction: the chapter is not a container for the story. It is the story made visible.
- Chapter 1 — *The Clearing* — field note layout, warm background pulse, drop cap, field sketch of the clearing on the first night
- Chapter 1 (Buried) — *The Same Night* — the unredacted first night; the other notebook; arrives after Chapter 1 with no signal; the pattern is not yet established

## [Chapter 2: The Map I Drew] — 2026-06-02

### Story

- Day 51. Age 12. Early autumn. He has named twelve things. The burial location on every map is deliberately wrong.
- Chapter 2 — *The Map I Drew* — the document layout: map as primary element, named territory as annotation grid, stats block, pull quote. The first time the design holds information the prose won't. The place annotations say more than the field note does. *The Place Where I Buried Something* is accent-highlighted. He knows what it is. He is not ready.
