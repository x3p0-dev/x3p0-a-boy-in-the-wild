Use the `abitw-sketch-briefs` skill before writing anything.

## Read first

- `.claude/docs/private/story-outline.md` — the outline entry for chapter $1,
  including any design events or fixed image placements.
- `.claude/docs/private/story-continuity.md` — what has been depicted already.

## Produce

For each sketch the chapter needs, produce the three outputs the skill
defines:

1. A Photoshop AI prompt.
2. A caption in his voice.
3. Alt text in his voice.

Captions and alt text are text the reader encounters, so they follow
`abitw-story-voice` — use it. Decide how many sketches the chapter genuinely
needs from the outline and content; do not pad.
