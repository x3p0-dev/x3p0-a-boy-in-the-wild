Use the `abitw-chapter-design` skill before writing anything — it defines the
framework, the variation system, and the recurring elements.

## Read first

- `.claude/docs/private/story-outline.md` — the outline entry for chapter
  $1: what happens, season, time, location, arc, fixed beats.
- `.claude/docs/private/story-continuity.md` — design decisions already made,
  so this chapter sits in continuity with what came before.

## Produce the brief

Write a design brief for chapter $1 that answers, for this specific chapter,
"what is it like to be inside it?" Work from the four sources the skill
defines — the content, the world, the arc, and any one-off moment that earns
its own treatment — and decide:

- The style variation (or a justification for a one-off treatment).
- Colour, typography emphasis, layout, and spacing intent.
- Movement: any canvas effect, and what it represents.
- Sound: whether the chapter wants ambient sound, music, or silence.
- Recurring elements present and how they appear.

Recommend, don't enumerate options. Ground every choice in this chapter's
needs, not in "other chapters do X." Stop at the brief — do not build the
pattern unless the user says go.
